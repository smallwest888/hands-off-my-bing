# Store listing draft

**Chinese name:** 别动我的Bing主页  
**English name:** Hands Off My Bing  
**German interface name:** Hands Off My Bing

**Short description:** Open Bing in Chrome’s new tab and apply your homepage choices automatically.

Hands Off My Bing opens Bing in Chrome’s new tab and applies your selected homepage display preferences automatically. Choose whether to show, hide, or leave the menu bar, news, trending carousel, and background image unchanged. The extension offers Chinese, English, and German interfaces. Pausing the extension does not restore Chrome’s default new-tab page; disabling or uninstalling it does. Bing’s own network activity and native settings remain handled by Bing.

The extension works only on the HTTPS root pages of `www.bing.com`, `cn.bing.com`, and `bing.com`. It uses limited permissions and includes no ads, analytics, or remote code. It is an independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Microsoft, Bing, or Google.
